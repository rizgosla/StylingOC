## Hero pattern
Headline comes FIRST, on white, then the plate. The page opens with a thin sticky header ("Journal" in serif, then a row of tiny caps section links), then a centred serif headline about 30px in two or three lines (max width about 750px), a centred serif date ("July 22nd, 2026"), then a full-width hairline bar with "PREVIOUS STORY" and "NEXT STORY" in tiny tracked caps at the far left and right. Directly under that bar sits the hero: a full-bleed landscape photo (Open House, approx 1900x1000, 16:9-ish crop) or a full-bleed 16:9 video embed (Unlocked). There is no caption on the hero. No kicker on the article page itself (the category tags "HOMES", "OPEN HOUSE", "FILM" appear only on index cards as tiny underlined caps). Under the hero, at the text column's left edge, a two-line credit block in small serif: "Words Sophie Sims" / "Photography Rachel Ferriman" with the names in italic. Then the standfirst: a large serif paragraph, about 24px, regular weight, sentence case, left-aligned, four to seven lines, with inline underlined links. Byline is therefore above the standfirst rather than under the headline. Headline never touches the image.

## Body column
Single centred column about 930px wide at 1900 (672px in the 0.72-scale shot), left-aligned ragged right. Two sizes: the standfirst at about 24px (roughly 70 characters per line) and the body at a noticeably small serif, about 16px, roughly 105-110 characters per line, which reads long. No drop cap. First body paragraph is set like the rest; the standfirst does the lede work. Paragraph gaps are a full blank line. Body colour is a warm near-black on a warm off-white ground (the site background is a faint cream, not pure white).

## Image rhythm
Very regular: standfirst, then a 2-up pair; one or two paragraphs; a single wide plate; two paragraphs; a 2-up pair; one paragraph; a single plate; and so on to the end. Pairs are two plates of equal width with a gutter of about 40px, spanning roughly 1230px at 1900, i.e. wider than the text column on both sides by about 150px; ratios are mixed (landscape exterior next to portrait of owners, both cropped to the same height so tops and bottoms align). Singles are landscape, about 1680px wide at 1900 (contained with page margins, not quite full-bleed), taller than the pairs. The Unlocked piece also stacks two pairs back to back (four plates) before the next paragraph. No image-beside-text; text is always its own band. NO captions anywhere in the body, and no credits on individual images; the single credit block under the hero covers the whole set. Between plates and text there is generous white space (about 60px).

## Quote / caption
No pull quotes. Quotes stay inline in the body with curly quotation marks. There are no captions at all, so the only caption-like elements are the credit block ("Words / Photography", small serif, italic names) under the hero and, on the feature, a closing italic paragraph inviting YouTube subscription. At the foot: a listing card (image, address, "view listing" link) for the sale, then a "RELATED ON THE MODERN HOUSE" section in tracked caps with a grid of image + tags + serif title + date. The prev/next bar at the top uses the only hairline rule in the article; the sticky nav has a second hairline underneath it.

## Adoptable / not
- [ADOPT] Headline-first-then-plate opener: pure type on white above a full-bleed plate, no text over the image, no kicker clutter.
- [ADAPT: add a caption] TMH runs its hero uncaptioned; the target rule wants every photo as a captioned plate, so keep the placement but add the tiny caption line under the plate.
- [ADOPT] Credit block "Words / Photography" with italic names as a small serif stack under the hero. This can be the byline pattern for photo essays.
- [ADAPT: set as small caps sans] The large serif standfirst is the right scale and length (four to seven lines, about 24px), but the target spec wants small caps sans; keep the size, swap the face.
- [ADOPT] 2-up pairs wider than the text column, equal width, equal height, mixed ratios, top-aligned. This is the strongest photo-essay device on the site.
- [ADOPT] Alternation pair / paragraph(s) / wide single / paragraph(s) as the default photo-essay rhythm.
- [ADAPT: cap at about 70-80 cpl] Body measure is 105-110 characters per line because the body type is small; either raise the body size or narrow the column.
- [ADAPT: captions required] The uncaptioned run of plates is elegant but violates the captioned-plate rule; the compromise is a single caption line per pair (left plate / right plate separated by a semicolon) so the rhythm stays quiet.
- [ADOPT] PREVIOUS STORY / NEXT STORY as tiny tracked caps on a single hairline bar, no arrows, no icons.
- [ADOPT] Hairline rule under the sticky nav and around the prev/next bar; only two rule weights used site-wide.
- [REJECT: no boxes/cards] The "view listing" card at the foot and the bordered "READ MORE" button on the index are boxed elements.
- [REJECT: text never over photos] The journal index hero puts the headline and a bordered READ MORE button on top of the photo with a light scrim; the article pages themselves avoid this, so copy the article, not the index.
- [ADOPT] Warm off-white ground with near-black serif; no colour other than photographs.

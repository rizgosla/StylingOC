## Hero pattern
Plate ABOVE the headline, centred, on a warm off-white page (not pure white; roughly the colour of unbleached paper). The hero image is a single landscape photograph about 680px wide on a 1400px render (a little under half the viewport), 5:4, with no caption and no credit. A tiny wide-tracked breadcrumb "HOME / DEPARTMENTS / Q+A" sits top-left above it in grey sans caps. Under the plate, left-aligned to the text column, is the date in numerals "04.22.25" in small sans; then, centred, the department label "Q+A" in small sans caps; then the headline in a geometric sans (Founders Grotesk-like), about 30px, medium weight, centred, three lines; then "by Deborah Shapiro" in regular sans, centred. No standfirst or dek at all; the long headline carries the dek's job ("Crumpled Silver & Pillowy Stone: This Cult Favorite Jeweler's First Furniture Collection Explores Some Familiar Themes"). No portrait lead; the hero is an installation shot of the furniture. The note uses the identical hero: plate above, date, "CURRENT OBSESSION" label, headline, "by Jill Singer".

## Body column
Single centred column about 610px wide, sans-serif body at about 16px with open leading, roughly 85-90 characters per line, left-aligned rag. Centred on the page (equal margins). No drop cap. The intro is two ordinary paragraphs of the writer's prose, same size and weight as everything else, followed immediately by an image row and then the first bold question; no rule and no typographic change marks the transition from intro to Q&A. Links in the body are underlined in the text colour.

## Interview structure
Questions are BOLD sans paragraphs with no name, no initials, no label; answers are regular-weight paragraphs directly beneath, with no prefix either. The interviewee's name never repeats; the reader infers the voices from bold versus regular. A multi-paragraph answer is simply several regular paragraphs in a row. Exchange spacing equals ordinary paragraph spacing (about one line), so the bold question is the only rhythm marker. Images are inserted every two or three exchanges as rows: sometimes three portrait images side by side (each 728x910 source, shown about 220px wide), sometimes a pair, sometimes one full-column image; none of the images inside the Q+A carry captions. No pull quotes. Closing device: the last exchange is followed directly by a final image row, then a "Tags:" line (jewelry, London, Milan Design Week 2025, silver), then "Previous Story / Next Story" links and a "Related Stories" block of three cards. No "edited for clarity" line, no credits block.

## Note structure
The note uses exactly the same template as the Q+A: same hero size, same date-label-headline-byline stack, same column, same type sizes. The only difference is the department label ("CURRENT OBSESSION" instead of "Q+A") and the length. Lead image: yes. Measure: identical. Two paragraphs, about 430 words, no subheads, no drop cap. After the text comes a vertical run of nine full-column images, each with a caption; the writer's second paragraph points to them ("See my texts ... below"), so the image run is the second half of the piece rather than an afterthought. Closing device: Tags line, Previous/Next links. There is no lighter template; the short piece simply has fewer paragraphs.

## Quote / caption
No pull quotes on either page. Captions: italic sans, about 15px, left-aligned to the column, sitting directly BELOW each image with roughly one line of space; usually just an attribution ("Concorde and LS Gomma for Uppercut", "Laila Gohar for Marimekko") rather than a sentence. Images in the Q+A run uncaptioned; only the note's image sequence is captioned. Photo credits are not given.

## Adoptable / not
- [ADOPT] One template for interview and note, distinguished only by a small caps department label ("Q+A", "CURRENT OBSESSION"); this maps directly onto a Sanity "kind" field driving the label.
- [ADOPT] Plate above headline, no caption competing with the headline, generous white above and below the label stack.
- [ADOPT] Date-label-headline-byline stack, centred, with the date in numerals; the label is the closest match to our tiny wide-tracked caps labels.
- [ADOPT] Italic attribution-only captions under the note's image run: short, below the plate, left-aligned to the column.
- [ADAPT: use full names on the first exchange (as AD does) and then bold/regular] Bold questions with no prefix are elegant but the reader has to work out who is speaking; a one-time name prefix fixes that.
- [ADAPT: single captioned plates between exchanges rather than uncaptioned triptych rows] Three-up image rows at 220px each are too small to read as plates.
- [ADAPT: add a small caps sans standfirst] Sight Unseen has no dek and pushes everything into a very long headline; we should keep headlines short and give the summary to the standfirst.
- [ADAPT: keep the closing tags line as tiny caps text, drop the card grid] Related Stories are rendered as image cards.
- [REJECT: no icons] Search magnifier and account icons in the nav.
- [REJECT: no boxes/cards] The three-card "Related Stories" block and the boxed leaderboard ad.
- [REJECT: photos are captioned plates] Uncaptioned hero and uncaptioned Q+A images.

## Hero pattern
Interview: the plate sits BESIDE the headline. A black-and-white portrait, roughly 3:4, occupies the left quarter of the page (about 360px wide on a 1400px render, starting at the left margin, top aligned with the headline). The headline sits in the right column, starting at the horizontal centre of the page: line one is the series name in caps serif ("BEHIND THE SCENES"), line two is the subject's name in italic serif followed by the topic in roman ("Clynton Lowry on the art handler's load."), around 34px. There is no separate eyebrow; the series name IS the first line of the headline. No standfirst on this piece. There is no caption on the portrait. Around 200px of white sits between the nav and the headline, and the meta row is pinned to the bottom edge of the portrait: far left "ISSUE 46, DIRECTORY   ARTS & CULTURE" in small sans caps, then in the right column "Words LOUISE BENSON / Photograph GIONCARLO VALENTINE" (role in bold sans, name in serif caps), and at the far right "SHARE FB PN X" as plain text links. Dates are not shown anywhere; the issue number stands in for the date. The Barber Osgerby piece uses the same grid with a landscape studio photo above a caption line ("Edward Barber (seated) and Jay Osgerby (standing) at their studio in East London.") and a one-line dek ("The London design duo on 30 years of friendship and collaboration."), so a dek exists in the template but is optional.

Note: the headline is centred ABOVE everything, with no plate beside it: "HOW TO ASK FOR A FAVOR" in caps serif (about 30px) and a roman serif subhead "Guidance on good deeds." directly under it, both centred, sitting in about 250px of white. The meta row (issue/section, category, Words/Photo credits, share) then runs full width on one line, and the two-column body begins below it: plate left (colour, 3:4, about 430px wide), text right.

Portrait leads: yes on the interview, cropped head-and-shoulders, subject looking slightly off camera, monochrome; never cropped to a square or a circle.

## Body column
The text column starts at the page's horizontal centre and runs to the right margin: about 650px wide at a 1400px render, serif (a Caslon-like text face) at roughly 19px with a generous leading, so about 70-75 characters per line. Offset right, not centred. No drop cap. Paragraphs are set with a first-line indent AND a blank line between them (belt and braces). The intro is plain body text, not italic or larger; footnote-style superscript numerals appear inline ("turning.1") and the footnotes themselves are rendered as bracketed sidenotes "( 1 ) ..." in the scrape (they appear as marginal notes on the print-derived layout). The interview's intro is two paragraphs: one scene-setting paragraph and one sentence of series framing ("This interview is the first in a new series ..."). No rule separates the intro from the Q&A; it flows straight in.

## Interview structure
Questions are marked with the interviewer's FULL NAME and a colon ("Louise Benson: Why did you start ..."), answers with the interviewee's full name and a colon ("Clynton Lowry: I started the magazine ..."). Neither is bold, italic, small-capped, or ruled; the names are set in the same serif weight as the running text, and only the first-line indent and the paragraph gap separate the exchanges. Question and answer are separate indented paragraphs with the same gap as any other paragraph. Whether the names repeat on every exchange could not be verified because the paywall cuts in after the first answer; the scrape of the print-derived Kinfolk pieces suggests they do. No inline images between exchanges on this piece; the single portrait stays fixed in the left column while the text scrolls. No pull quotes. Closing device could not be seen (paywall), but the sidenotes footnote apparatus and the credits row at the top mean the piece carries no trailing credits.

## Note structure
Headline scale is smaller than the interview (30px caps vs 34px) and it is centred rather than left-aligned in the right column, which is the main signal that this is a "Starters" item rather than a feature. Lead image: yes, a single colour plate in the left column, no caption. Measure: identical to the interview column. Three paragraphs, roughly 450 words, plain serif, no drop cap, no subheads, no pull quote. Closing device: none; the page fades into the subscription prompt and then the "FREE PREVIEW" related-stories block. The note shares the same two-column grid and type scale as the feature; only the header block differs (centred title above the meta row rather than title beside the plate).

## Quote / caption
No pull quotes appear on either page. Captions, where present (Barber Osgerby), are a single line of small roman serif directly under the plate, no label, no rule. Photo credit is carried in the meta row as "Photograph NAME" rather than under the image. Footnote sidenotes are set in the body face, prefixed with a bracketed numeral.

## Adoptable / not
- [ADOPT] Plate beside headline for interviews: portrait left quarter, headline in the right half, text on white throughout; nothing sits on the photograph.
- [ADOPT] Series name as the first line of the headline in caps, subject name italic on line two: a cheap way to signal "In conversation" without an icon or a box.
- [ADOPT] Credits as a labelled row ("Words NAME / Photograph NAME") pinned to the base of the plate; roles in small caps sans reads as our tiny wide-tracked label style.
- [ADOPT] Full-name speaker prefixes in the same weight as body text, relying on paragraph rhythm rather than bold; this is the quietest Q&A marking seen across the four sites.
- [ADAPT: keep the two-column grid but put the standfirst in small caps sans under the headline; Kinfolk's dek is roman serif and often omitted] Dek treatment.
- [ADAPT: drop the first-line indent and keep only the paragraph gap, matching AD] Paragraph spacing.
- [ADAPT: use our light hairline under the meta row instead of pure whitespace] The meta row currently floats with no rule; a hairline would anchor it without adding a box.
- [ADAPT: centred headline for notes is worth copying, but keep the note's plate captioned] Note header.
- [REJECT: no icons] The "FB PN X" share links are plain text here, which is fine, but the hamburger menu and cart icon in the nav would need to become words.
- [REJECT: text never over photos / no scrims] The dark subscription bar that slides over the article bottom is a scrimmed overlay; our paywall-free site simply omits it.
- [REJECT: photos are captioned plates] Kinfolk leaves the note plate uncaptioned; we should caption every plate.

## Hero pattern
The "hero" is a single centred plate with the headline in the site header rather than above the body. The header bar holds the CEREAL wordmark left, "Search / Information" links, the article title "SCHINDLER HOUSE" centred in small caps serif (about 16px), and "Menu" right, with a hairline rule under the bar. Below that, the page is a three-column frame: a narrow left metadata column ("Photos:" / "Rich Stapleton", "Item:" / "(180-02)", and "Previous / Next" text links, all tiny sans), the plate centred in the middle (portrait about 550x700 at the 0.72 screenshot scale, i.e. roughly 760x970 at 1900; landscape plates are shown at the same width), and a right rail separated by a vertical hairline holding a filmstrip of thumbnails labelled "(180-02)", "(180-03)" and so on. The ground is a pale warm cream, not white. No caption on the plate; no kicker; no dek at this point. The title never sits on the image. Scrolling past the plate reveals a centred subtitle "100 YEARS IN THE MAKING" in tracked caps serif (about 12px), then a row with "Words: Erik Benjamins" and "Keywords: Architecture, Art, California, Exhibition, Los Angeles" (underlined tags) in the left columns, and the essay starting in the middle column on the same baseline.

## Body column
Middle column about 840px wide at 1900 (609px in the screenshot), JUSTIFIED, small serif at about 15px with generous leading, roughly 105-115 characters per line. No drop cap. First paragraph is set like the others; the subtitle in tracked caps above it acts as the only lede device. Paragraph gaps are a full line. Body colour is warm near-black on cream. The essay is long (eight paragraphs) and unbroken by images; all plates live in the viewer above / in the filmstrip.

## Image rhythm
Zero images inside the text. The article is structured as plate-sequence-then-essay: twelve numbered plates (180-02 to 180-13) are browsed one at a time in the viewer via Previous / Next or by clicking thumbnails in the right filmstrip, and the essay follows below. Plates are shown one per view, centred, constant width, portrait and landscape mixed, black-and-white and colour mixed. Index pages (e.g. /tag/interiors) show the plates as a loose grid of small thumbnails with the item number "(073-05)" in tiny sans above a bold serif title, tiles of mixed heights placed unsystematically ("Sort by: Unsystematic"). No image-beside-text.

## Quote / caption
No pull quotes and no captions. The only per-image text is the item number "(180-05)" and the photographer credit in the metadata column. The subtitle "100 YEARS IN THE MAKING" is the one display-type moment: tracked caps, serif, centred over the essay column, small. The end of the essay closes with an italic underlined link ("makcenter.org/exhibitions"). Rules: a hairline under the header, a vertical hairline separating the filmstrip rail, and a hairline under the index filter block; all the same weight.

## Adoptable / not
- [ADOPT] Plate as a centred object on a quiet ground with wide margins either side; the photo is treated as a print, not a banner.
- [ADOPT] Metadata column left of the plate: "Photos:" / "Words:" labels in tiny sans with the name below, and "Item:" numbering. This is a clean way to credit without captions cluttering each image.
- [ADAPT: add a caption line] Cereal plates have no captions; keep the numbered-plate idea but add the short caption under the plate as the rule requires.
- [ADOPT] Tracked-caps subtitle centred above the essay as the lede device; maps to the tiny wide-tracked caps label.
- [ADOPT] Keywords as underlined text tags in the left column, no pills or chips.
- [ADAPT: left-align] The justified small serif is authentically magazine-like but at 105-115 characters per line it is hard to read on screen; use ragged-right and a 70-80 cpl measure.
- [ADAPT: interleave a few plates] Cereal separates all plates from the essay; for the target photo-essay template keep the sequence idea (numbered plates, one per band) but let the essay run between groups of plates instead of after all of them.
- [ADOPT] Previous / Next as plain text links, no arrows, no icons.
- [ADOPT] Vertical hairline as the only divider between the plate column and the filmstrip; no boxes.
- [REJECT: no boxes/cards] The right-rail thumbnail filmstrip is effectively a card column and a UI pattern rather than editorial layout; do not reproduce it.
- [ADOPT] Warm cream ground and a single near-black ink; no accent colour.
- [ADOPT] Small-caps serif article title in the header bar is a nice secondary place for the title, but the template still needs a full-size headline above the first plate.

## Hero pattern
Plate ABOVE the headline, full-bleed portrait, roughly 16:10 crop of a head-and-shoulders photograph (Little Wing Lee smiling against a painted wall). The caption sits directly under the image, left-aligned to the page margin, in small grey sans: descriptive caption first, then the credit "Photo: Kelly Marshall" after a wider gap on the same line. The header block below is on a very pale grey band and is entirely centred: a red section label "The Source" (a newsletter name; on the Meier page the label is "ARCHITECTURE + DESIGN" in tracked caps and the headline itself begins "Q+A:"), a bold condensed sans headline at about 44px on two lines, a bold sans dek at about 17px, a circular author avatar, "By Mel Studach" and the date "June 18, 2024" in small grey sans. Portrait leads: yes, and cropped close; the subject's face occupies the top third of the viewport.

## Body column
Centred column about 620px wide, serif text at about 19px, roughly 70 characters per line, with a right rail reserved for advertising. No drop cap on either interview. The intro on the PRO piece begins with an italic editorial note ("This is an edition of The Source newsletter ..."), then a hairline rule, then one first-person intro paragraph. On the Meier page the intro is one long third-person paragraph that ends with a framing sentence naming the meeting place; no rule separates it from the first question. Paragraphs are separated by space, no indent.

## Interview structure
Two conventions on the same site.
- Meier (formal Q+A): the first exchange uses full names in bold, with the interviewer's name in bold ITALIC ("Architectural Digest:") and the interviewee's in bold roman ("Richard Meier:"); every later exchange uses bold italic "AD:" and bold "RM:". Question text and answer text are both regular weight after the prefix. Each exchange is two short paragraphs with a normal paragraph gap; ten exchanges; no images between them on this page; the only hairline rules on the page bracket inserted ad and "Trending video" units, not editorial breaks. Closing device: two link lines (slide show, video).
- Little Wing Lee (newsletter conversation): the first exchange uses bold FIRST names ("Mel:" / "Little Wing:"); afterwards questions are bold paragraphs with no prefix and answers are regular paragraphs with no prefix (the Sight Unseen convention). One column-width image sits between exchanges 3 and 4 with a caption plus credit. The final question is a set-piece ("For a few wise words to close, what is the best business advice you've ever received?"). Closing device: italic "This interview has been condensed and edited for brevity and clarity."
Neither piece uses pull quotes.

No pull quotes. Captions are small grey sans directly under the plate, left-aligned, caption then credit on one line separated by a gap; the credit is prefixed "Photo:" or "Photography courtesy".

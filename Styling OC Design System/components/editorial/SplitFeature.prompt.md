Split feature — used for the two service lines on Home and for interleaved editorial passages.

```jsx
<SplitFeature image="/assets/images/interior-living-marble.jpeg" numeral="01" eyebrow="Interior Design"
  title="Full-service residential design" body={["…"]} caption="Newport Coast residence" imageSide="left" offset="top" />
```

Alternate `imageSide` between consecutive splits. Keep `offset` off-center — vertical centering flattens the editorial rhythm.

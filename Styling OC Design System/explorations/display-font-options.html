Fashion-masthead thin. Nearly monoline caps with tall, narrow letterforms. The most editorial, the least sturdy.

My pick for "less fat" without losing the magazine feel: Prata. It keeps the Didone contrast that makes the wordmark work but sits noticeably lighter. Cormorant is lighter still and reads warmer and more traditional; Italiana is the thinnest but its caps are narrow enough that long headlines get tight, and it has only one weight.
